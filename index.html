Privacy Policy

Application: Descent Aid (com.killa.descentaid)

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Descent Aid is an offline dice-rolling utility. The application is completely local and does not require an account or internet connection to function.

Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, request, access, or store any personal information (e.g., name, email address, location, contacts, or photos).
  • No Device Data: The application does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or hardware information.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: The application contains no analytics tools, crash reporting libraries, advertising frameworks, or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing and Transfer

Because no data is collected, no user data is ever sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties.

Application Lifecycle

Descent Aid has been retired and is slated for permanent removal from the Google Play Store.
